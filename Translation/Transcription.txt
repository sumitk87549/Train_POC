STRUCTURE RULES (VERY IMPORTANT FOR AUDIO):

1. Break long sentences into smaller natural spoken units
2. Each sentence should be easy to speak in one breath
3. Prefer 8–16 word sentences (for smooth listening)
4. Add natural pause points using commas or sentence breaks
5. Avoid long, complex, academic sentence structures

6. Convert formal narration into spoken-style flow
   Example:
   "He realized that he had made a mistake"
   → "Usse tab samajh aaya… ki usne galti kar di"

7. Maintain continuity between sentences (no abrupt jumps)
8. Avoid over-short sentences unless emotional or dialogue
